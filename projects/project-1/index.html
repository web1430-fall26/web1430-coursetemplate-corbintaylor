Why Ride E-Bikes

Ready to reduce your Carbon footprint? Ready to save money on gas, maintenance, and parking? Or do you just want to go for a smooth, effortless ride? Then an electric bike is for you!

Tips for Riding

Here are some helpful tips:

  1. Wear a helmet.
  2. Don't look down; look where you are going.
  3. Be aware. Look for people and cars around you.
  4. Make sure your kick-stand is up before riding.
  5. Wear reflective gear.
